## Summary

Prani Doctor helps pet and livestock owners book veterinary services and manage farm records. This document mirrors the platform privacy policy for Play Store and in-app linking.

**Key points:**

- We collect account, location hierarchy, animal/farm data, device info, and service history.
- We do **not** sell personal data.
- AI features require separate consent and may use third-party LLM providers.
- Contact support@pranidoctor.com for access or deletion requests.

For the full policy, open **Settings → Privacy Policy** in the app or visit the public URL above.
